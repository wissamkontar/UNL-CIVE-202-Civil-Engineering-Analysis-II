Built For Civil Engineers

CIVE 202 teaches Python through civil engineering problems, not generic coding exercises. Students work with datasets and project prompts connected to environmental, structural, transportation, infrastructure, and geotechnical engineering.

Real Project Workflow

Students practice the full data analysis process: organizing data, analyzing results, creating visuals, interpreting findings, and integrating everything into a client-ready engineering deliverable.

Professional Communication

The course emphasizes more than code. Students learn how to explain assumptions, document methods, annotate code, and turn technical results into reports that clients and engineering teams can understand.
