ABOUT CIVE 202

Python, data analysis, and civil engineering practice in one course.

CIVE 202, Civil Engineering Analysis II, is a sophomore-level course in the University of Nebraska-Lincoln Civil Engineering curriculum. The course helps students build Python and data analysis skills through real civil engineering applications.

Rather than treating coding as a separate subject, CIVE 202 places Python inside the engineering workflow. Students respond to client-style prompts, work with real datasets, create technical visuals, troubleshoot code, and communicate findings through professional reports.

The course is part of UNL’s Civil Engineering Design Spine, which connects students to major-specific courses every semester while building practical skills for future engineering work.
