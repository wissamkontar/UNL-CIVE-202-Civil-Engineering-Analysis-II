Client Prompt

Client Prompt

A coalition of agencies is evaluating water quality from Lake Sakakawea and the McClusky Canal in western North Dakota. The proposed project would pump water through a pipeline across the state to the Sheyenne River near Lake Ashtabula.

Before the pipeline can be approved, the client needs to understand whether water from the end of the McClusky Canal could affect the receiving water in the Sheyenne River and Lake Ashtabula. Students will analyze major ions, solids, mass loadings, and water quality trends to support the client’s decision-making.

Client Requests

  1. Determine whether water quality degrades from Lake Sakakawea to the end of the McClusky Canal.
  2. Identify which water quality parameters are most concerning and explain why.
  3. Estimate the total dissolved solids concentration expected in Lake Ashtabula after mixing, assuming pipeline water quality matches the McClusky Canal outlet.
  4. Build the analysis so flow rate and concentration values can be changed to test multiple scenarios.
  5. Create boxplots for at least four water quality parameters across McClusky Canal sampling locations.
  6. Calculate mean, median, maximum, and minimum values for each sample point and water quality parameter of interest.

Deliverables and Requirements

Deliverables

  • Cleaned and organized water quality data files used in the Python analysis.
  • A Python notebook or code file documenting the full analysis workflow.
  • Python functions used to calculate mass loading, mixing, or scenario-based water quality outputs.
  • At least two appropriate visualizations, including required boxplots for selected water quality parameters.
  • A scope of work describing the analysis tasks completed for the client.
  • A Gantt chart showing project tasks, responsibilities, dates, and task bars.
  • A timesheet documenting hours spent on each major project activity.
  • Technical and non-technical executive summaries.
  • A written report summarizing findings and recommendations for the client.

Report Requirements

  • A discussion of whether water quality changes from Lake Sakakawea to the end of the McClusky Canal.
  • Identification of the most concerning water quality parameters, with an explanation of why those parameters are significant.
  • A clear explanation of the mixing or mass balance method used to estimate water quality in Lake Ashtabula.
  • Tables or figures showing summary statistics for selected water quality parameters by sampling location.
  • An analysis of how water quality in the McClusky Canal could be improved to help ensure good quality water reaches Lake Ashtabula.
  • References and citations for all data sources and supporting research used in the report.
