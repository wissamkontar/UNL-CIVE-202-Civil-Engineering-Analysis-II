Project 5F

Midwest Travel Behavior Analysis

Students use NHTS datasets to investigate travel behavior patterns across households, people, vehicles, and trips in the Midwest.

Category Transportation Engineering
Focus Skill Integrate Data Analysis Skills
Tools Python, Pandas, Visualization, NHTS Data
01

Client Prompt

Client Prompt

A coalition of Midwestern Departments of Transportation is interested in understanding travel behavior patterns across the region. The National Household Travel Survey provides detailed information about households, people, vehicles, and trips, making it a useful dataset for transportation planning and policy analysis.

Students will use the NHTS datasets to develop at least two research questions about Midwest travel behavior. Each question must require information from multiple datasets, encouraging students to clean, merge, filter, and analyze cross-level transportation data.

Client Requests

  1. Focus the analysis on the Midwest region using the 2022 National Household Travel Survey data.
  2. Develop at least two research questions related to travel behavior.
  3. Make sure each research question uses variables from multiple NHTS datasets, such as household, person, vehicle, and trip-level data.
  4. Complete a quantitative analysis for the first research question using appropriate summary tables or visualizations.
  5. Complete a quantitative analysis for the second research question using appropriate summary tables or visualizations.
  6. Interpret patterns in the results and explain why those patterns matter for transportation planning or policy.

Deliverables and Requirements

Deliverables

  • Cleaned and organized NHTS data files used in the Python analysis.
  • A Python notebook or code file documenting the full analysis workflow.
  • Python functions used to clean, merge, filter, summarize, or visualize the data.
  • At least two research questions related to Midwest travel behavior.
  • Summary tables, statistics, or visualizations that support each research question.
  • A scope of work describing the analysis tasks completed for the client.
  • A Gantt chart showing project tasks, responsibilities, dates, and task bars.
  • A timesheet documenting hours spent on each major project activity.
  • Technical and non-technical executive summaries.
  • A written report summarizing findings and transportation planning insights.

Report Requirements

  • Clearly state the two research questions being investigated.
  • Explain the rationale for each research question and why it matters for transportation planning or policy.
  • Identify the NHTS variables used for each question and explain why those variables are relevant.
  • Describe how the NHTS datasets were processed, filtered, cleaned, and merged.
  • Present analysis results for each question using figures, plots, summary statistics, or tables.
  • Interpret observed patterns and explain what the results suggest about travel behavior in the Midwest.
  • References and citations for all datasets, documentation, and supporting research used in the report.
