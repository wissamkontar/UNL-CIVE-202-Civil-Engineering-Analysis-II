Project 5B

Drinking Water Violations Across States

Students compare Total Coliform Rule violations across five states and examine how drinking water compliance patterns differ between small and large water systems.

Category Environmental Engineering
Focus Skill Integrate Data Analysis Skills
Tools Python, Pandas, Functions, Visualization
01

Client Prompt

Client Prompt

A research team at UMass Amherst is studying Total Coliform Rule violations in public drinking water systems across the United States. Your team has been hired to help analyze Safe Drinking Water Information System violation reports from five states located in different EPA regions.

The client wants to better understand how TCR violations vary across states and whether small water systems experience different violation patterns than larger systems. Students will use Python to clean, organize, summarize, and visualize violation data in a way that supports a professional research report.

Client Requests

  1. Select five states from different EPA regions to create a representative comparison of TCR violations.
  2. Download and organize SDWIS violation report data for the selected states.
  3. Create a summary table or figure showing TCR violations in each state over time.
  4. Investigate whether small water systems have more TCR violations than larger water systems.
  5. Compare results across states and explain possible reasons for observed differences.

Deliverables and Requirements

Deliverables

  • Cleaned and organized SDWIS violation data files used in the Python analysis.
  • A Python notebook or code file documenting the full analysis workflow.
  • Python functions used to clean, filter, summarize, or visualize the violation data.
  • At least two appropriate graphs or summary tables that support the client’s questions.
  • A scope of work describing the analysis tasks completed for the client.
  • A Gantt chart showing project tasks, responsibilities, dates, and task bars.
  • A timesheet documenting hours spent on each major project activity.
  • Technical and non-technical executive summaries.
  • A written report summarizing findings and recommendations for the client.

Report Requirements

  • A discussion of differences in TCR violations across the five selected states.
  • An explanation of possible reasons for differences across states, supported by appropriate research or references.
  • A comparison of TCR violations between small and large water systems.
  • Tables or figures that clearly communicate violation trends over time.
  • A methods section explaining how SDWIS data were selected, cleaned, filtered, grouped, and analyzed.
  • References and citations for all data sources and supporting research used in the report.
